## Full description (max 4000)

Cosmo Strike is an arcade space shooter built around one idea: fly close, chain hard and never stop firing. A landscape shoot 'em up with handcrafted levels, telegraphed boss fights and a daily run you can master.

A HANDCRAFTED CAMPAIGN
12 choreographed levels across 4 alien biomes. Enemy formations sweep in on scripted paths, terrain corridors squeeze shut around you, and every level ends in a multi-phase boss fight where each attack is telegraphed before it lands.

CLOSE-RANGE COMBAT
• Kill chains drive a ×1–×4 score multiplier
• Graze bullets at point-blank range to charge Overdrive
• Trigger Overdrive for a 5-second piercing beam that melts enemy fire
• Wipe out whole formations for bonus score and pickups
• Draft one of three upgrades after every level clear

8 FLIGHT MODES
Sortie, Cruise, Afterburn, Swarm, Holdout, Blitz, Overload and Knife Edge each change the rules, from a relaxed Cruise to one-hit Knife Edge runs.

A REASON TO FLY EVERY DAY
• Daily Directives: clear all 3 for a bonus and keep your streak alive
• Daily Run: one seeded run per day, the same for every pilot
• Weekly Ops, Sector Cups and the Frontline season track
• 72 Commendations and global, weekly and squadron Standings

THE HANGAR
Spend credits on permanent ship upgrades and unlock new frames as you clear the campaign.

MAKE THE SHIP YOURS
Paint your hull with Liveries, fit engine Exhausts and recolour the battlefield with Sectors.

PLAY ANYWHERE
Fully playable offline, smooth 60 FPS on everyday phones and tablets. Progress saves on your device and syncs to the cloud when you sign in.

Free to play, with optional in-app purchases and an optional Strike Command subscription (monthly or annual): no ads, every Livery, Exhaust and Sector, 1.5× credits and the Frontline Command lane. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled, and can be managed in Google Play.

Lock in, pilot. The stars are waiting.
